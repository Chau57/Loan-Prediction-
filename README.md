This project was developed in the context of a credit risk analysis scenario, where the objective is to support a commercial bank in identifying high-risk borrowers and improving loan approval strategies.

The analysis began with an overview of the loan portfolio, revealing an average default rate of 11.61%. However, further segmentation showed that risk is not evenly distributed.

Through detailed analysis, I identified that certain borrower segments exhibit significantly higher default rates (up to 30–40%), particularly among young, low-income, and unemployed individuals.

Key risk drivers were also analyzed, including credit score, debt-to-income ratio, and employment stability, all of which showed strong relationships with default probability.

Based on these findings, I proposed targeted risk management strategies, such as stricter screening for high-risk segments and adjusting lending policies based on financial indicators.

The final output is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to support decision-makers in monitoring risk and optimizing loan approval processes.
